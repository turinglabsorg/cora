# Cora — Design System

The admin panel is a **radio studio mixing desk**. Each agent is a channel strip on the desk: LEDs for its state, a small monitor showing its desktop. Discord is the "ON AIR" lamp. The live activity feed is a teletype strip. Dark and tactile: one amber light on near-pure black, never brown.

Each agent also has its own `color` (stored on the agent, default `#e8a33d`), used for its picture and its name in the feed. Cora draws each agent's picture like the mark: a circle and a ring as lines in the agent's color on `#070707`, the ring's far half faint, two big eyes; the seed varies where the ring sits (visor, cap, collar), its tilt and the eyes. A drawn picture carries its own circle, so it shows with no ring around it (`.avatar.drawn`); an uploaded one gets the color ring.

## Typography

Loaded from Google Fonts.

- **Display — Outfit** (600): logo, page titles, panel titles, agent names on strips. Rounded geometric, sentence case, tight tracking (`-0.02em` small, `-0.04em` large).
- **Body — Manrope** (400/500/600): forms, paragraphs, buttons.
- **Mono — DM Mono** (400/500): cron expressions, ids, timestamps, the teletype feed, small hardware-style labels (`font-size: 10–11px`, uppercase, `letter-spacing: .12em`).

Scale: 11 / 13 / 15 (base) / 17 / 20 / 38 / 44 / 56px.

## Spacing & shape

- Spacing unit 4px; common steps 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- Radii: `--r-sm 6px` (inputs, chips), `--r-md 10px` (cards), `--r-lg 16px` (strips, panels). LEDs and avatars are circles.
- Borders are 1px `--line`; panels add an inner top highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.04)` and a soft drop shadow, like brushed hardware.
- Content max width 1280px, 16px side gutter on phones.

## Brand

- **Name:** Cora — Computer-Operating Resident Agents. Wordmark "Cora" in Outfit 600.
- **Mark:** a sphere in outline on black: a thin amber rim and one equator tilted −24°, its far half at 38% opacity for depth. No fill, no glow. Drawn by `bin/icons.mjs` (PNG app icons, supersampled) and `web/favicon.svg`.
- **Login:** the mark, the wordmark, the tagline, then the password card; nothing animated around it.

## App shell

The panel behaves like an installed app (web manifest, apple-touch-icon, no pinch or double-tap zoom, 16px inputs on phones so iOS never zooms).

- **Desktop (≥ 900px)**: a fixed sidebar (brand, the channel card with the ON AIR lamp, navigation, the agents with a status dot, and at its foot who is signed in, which opens the account menu) and a scrolling content column.
- **Phone**: a top app bar (back chevron on sub-pages, page title, and at the right end your account: a 36px round button with your mark, which opens the account menu) and a bottom tab bar (Agenti, Banco, Brain, Artefatti, Impostazioni: one row, as many equal columns as there are sections) with safe-area padding. The shell is `position: fixed` and sized to the real visible viewport via `--app-h` (set from `visualViewport` in `fitViewport()`), so iOS standalone PWAs show no dead strip at the bottom; only the content scrolls. While the keyboard is up (`.kbd-open`) the tab bar hides so the composer rests on the keyboard. Chat pages fill the screen with the composer docked above the tab bar: there the agent card is hidden (the app bar already names the agent), the working card is one line, and the tab bar keeps only the clearance the home indicator needs (`max(4px, safe-bottom - 14px)`) instead of the whole inset, so the log gets about two thirds of the screen.
- **Icons**: inline 24px stroke icons (`ICONS` in `web/app.js`, stroke 1.8, round caps), in amber icon wells (`.panel-ico`) on panel headers and stat cards; Discord is `#`, Telegram is the paper plane, Slack is four strokes turning around a centre.
- **Panels**: every section is a `.panel` with a header (icon well, title in the display font, one-line hint, actions on the right; on phones status badges drop under the title) and a body with 14px rhythm. An agent's forms end with a sticky `.savebar`; Settings has none, a setting saves as it changes.

## Components

- **Usage heatmaps**: show the first recorded usage day through today, at most the year returned by the API. Each agent starts at its own first use. Week alignment uses invisible placeholders, never dated cells before the first record or in the future. Keep squares at 14px; show the date range with its year. Scroll long histories to the latest week only after mounting, and realign when the viewport changes. Show an explicit empty state when there is no recorded usage.
- **Home**: opening the panel at `/` or `#/` shows Agents. Global token usage comes first, followed by the agent roster and individual token usage. The lounge has its own `#/salotto` route, also used by lounge requests and push notifications, but no menu entry: it opens from the Banco's *Salotto* button, the Banco entry stays lit while it is open, and on a phone its back chevron returns to the Banco. Explicit links to agent tabs still open the requested tab.
- **Boot outline**: while the state first loads, the shell draws the page in the address (`bootSkeleton()`): its own title and the sidebar entry lit, plain rows, the chat outline for the lounge and an agent; only the agents page keeps its usage and agent-card outline.
- **Skeletons**: neutral placeholders use the existing panel and line tokens with a quiet neutral pulse, never amber. Match the content shape: avatar and text rows, usage heatmaps, and alternating chat bubbles with a composer. Loading regions use `role="status"`, a translated loading label and `aria-busy="true"`; visual shapes are hidden from assistive technology. Replace placeholders with content or a concrete error, and respect reduced motion.

- **Token forms**: one password input and a «Collega» button in an `.input-row.token`. A service that needs two tokens (Slack: the bot's and the app-level one) stacks both inputs in a `.stack-sm` with the button on its own row, and says in the steps which page of the provider each one comes from.
- **Manifest block**: for a provider that creates its apps from a document (Slack), a row with «Copia il manifest» and the link that opens the provider, and a `<details>` holding the JSON in the mono font. Never make someone retype what a paste can do.
- **Telegram group guide** (`.telegram-setup`): inside Account Telegram, a group select and *Verifica passaggi* lead into five rows separated by hairlines. A 28px mono number becomes a green check only after actual evidence; the first unfinished step is amber, later steps neutral. Each row names its outcome and the next action, with links to BotFather, the group invitation, Settings or the agent's Listening and its own *Ho fatto, verifica* button. Status text distinguishes unchecked, incomplete and verified; errors clear previous success instead of implying it is current. The final step gives a copyable message with a unique test code for the person to send and distinguishes reception from the correlated delivered reply. Retain the re-invite instruction after a privacy change even when membership is confirmed. Keep controls at least 44px tall, wrap titles and buttons on phones, and update only the guide so profile drafts survive checks.

- **Your account** (`accountButton()`, `.profile-*`): the signed-in person's mark (`.user-mark`: the picture their sign-in gave, else their initials in amber DM Mono on `--panel-2`) in a round button at the phone top bar's right end, and their name and email at the sidebar's foot with a chevron. Either opens one menu, a `--panel` card with a `--line-strong` hairline and a deep shadow, below the button on a phone (over a 45% black scrim) and above the foot on a desktop (300px wide): the mark, name in the display font, email in mono and the role badge (*Owner* / *Utente*), then *Accesso*, *Lingua e notifiche*, a hairline and *Esci*. *Esci* never signs out at once: the menu turns into *Vuoi uscire?* with one line on what follows (on usecora.space you also leave the cloud and sign in again; locally the password is needed again), *Annulla* (ghost) and *Esci* (primary). Esc, a tap outside, navigation and a resize close the menu; opened from the keyboard it takes the focus.
- **ON AIR lamp** (`.onair`): in the sidebar's channel card on desktop; phones show a channel's state in Settings, and the top bar's right end is your account. Lit red with glow when Discord is `ready`, amber blinking while connecting, dark when off/error (error text in the tooltip and the dashboard).
- **Channel strip** (`.strip`): agent card on the dashboard. Top: monitor (desktop thumbnail, 16:10, scanline overlay, "SPENTO" when off). Middle: avatar (its color ring, or the drawn picture's own circle), name (display font), model (mono). Bottom: LEDs (`.led` on/off/warn) for *attivo*, *desktop*, *strumenti*. Whole strip is a link to the agent.
- **Teletype** (`.teletype`): paper strip with perforation edges, mono text, newest on top, each line `time · who · what`; tool calls in blue, errors in red, agent messages in the agent's color.
- **Tabs** (`.tabs`): hardware buttons; active one is lit amber with a small LED dot. Scroll horizontally on phones.
- **Buttons**: `.btn` (panel-2, line border), `.btn.primary` (amber fill, ink text), `.btn.danger` (red outline), `.btn.ghost`. 36px tall, 44px on touch.
- **Toggle** (`.switch`): small rocker switch with LED.
- **Model picker** (`.model-pick`): the model list grouped by provider, and under it a mono text box for any `provider/model` id; when the box is filled it wins and the list dims. Used while an agent is being created, where there is no Brain tab yet.
- **Brain** (`.brain-main`): the agent's Cervello tab, two columns on desktop and one on phones. Left: the harnesses as `.harness-row` buttons, the chosen one lit amber, each with its one-line job and a «da installare» badge when this machine does not have it. Right (`.brain-side`, separated by a hairline): a `.segmented` switch between **Su questa macchina** and **Nel cloud** — shown only when both exist — and under it the models for that pair as `.model-row`s. Local rows carry size, the card's verdict (`entra` / `stretta` / `non entra`) or, for a model that is loaded, what the card actually took; cloud rows carry the endpoint that answers. A row that cannot be used is disabled and says why: *senza strumenti*, or *questa macchina non è collegata a Ollama Cloud*. The instance default appears as the first row only in the harness and the place it belongs to. A harness this machine cannot run shows a `.notice.warn` with what it needs instead of a model list, and the free-text id lives behind a `.brain-custom` disclosure.
- **Notice** (`.notice`, `.notice.warn`): an inline explanation inside a panel, icon left and text right; the warn variant is amber-lined on an amber wash. For a condition the reader must act on, never for status.
- **Subscription picker** (`.subscription-picker`): a quiet account card with a provider mark, identity and a text status with an LED. The default resident account is identified explicitly; **Change account** reveals selectable account rows and **Connect another account**. Browser authorization replaces the card body with two numbered steps; device codes use a dashed mono well with a copy button, while Claude's returned code goes in a password field. Show errors inline, keep entered codes across polls, and place advanced device-code help behind a disclosure. Avoid showing creation inputs, selection controls and login actions simultaneously. The same component serves agent forms and Settings, with context-appropriate copy.
- **Settings** (`.rail`): one list on the left (272px, a `--panel` card whose items scroll inside it, so its 8px margin never scrolls away) and the page of the entry in the address on the right (`#/settings/<entry>`). The list is grouped under mono caps labels: *Canali* (Discord, Telegram, Slack), *Abbonamenti* (*Account del server*, Claude, ChatGPT / Codex, and Grok and Meta once installed or connected), *Chiavi API* (Ollama, OrcaRouter, DeepSeek, TypeSafe, Soniox), *Agenti* (Regia, Modelli e sonno, GitHub, Domini), *Persone* (Accesso, Chiavi SSH), *Generale* (Controlli di doc, Lingua e notifiche). Each entry is a 30px icon well, the name in 14px Manrope with its state in faint mono under it ("in uso · in onda", "2 account", "Chiave salvata", "11 messaggi · obiettivi accesi", "solo tu"), and an 8px LED for the entries that can work or not: green when it works here, amber for a program missing, a sign-in in progress or a default model that does not answer, red for a channel in error, dark otherwise; entries with nothing to light keep the space empty. The chosen entry is lit like the sidebar: `--panel-2` with a 3px amber inset bar, the name and its icon well amber, and the list scrolls to keep it in view. Without an entry in the address a desktop opens the primary platform. A platform's page starts with whether Cora is there: off, an amber *Accendi <platform>* (it joins the others); on with others, *Rendi principale* (ghost) and *Spegni* (armed twice); the primary says that Cora's private messages to the owner arrive there. An agent's *Ascolto* panel has one row per platform on, *Su <platform>*: a switch, and under it that platform's channel chips, hidden while the switch is off. Agent cards and the agent header carry one account badge per platform the agent is on (*Discord · Bot suo*), or *Solo pannello*. On phones the list comes first, full width, each entry with a chevron; an entry opens its page alone, with *‹ Impostazioni* above it. The old tab addresses (`collegamenti/<id>`, `canali`, `agenti`…) open their page. Every page stays mounted, so a sign-in half done survives a switch. On a desktop the page fits the screen (`.mode-rail`): the title stays, the list and the entry's page each scroll in their own column, the page returns to its top when the entry changes, and the margins above and below never scroll away; on phones the page scrolls as a whole.
- **New agent** (`#/new`): identity, character, listening and voice, followed by *Create agent*. Brain and subscription selection live only in the saved agent’s Brain tab; creation uses the instance default model and the normal computer default.
- **Setting rows** (`.set-row`, `setRow()`): the grammar of every settings page. What the setting is in 15px Manrope with one muted line under it (at most 64 characters wide) on the left; its control on the right: a rocker, a 92px mono number, an hour select, a badge, a primary button. A wide control (a model picker, a list of switches, an ids field, a token form with its steps) goes under the text (`.stack`). Rows sit between hairlines inside a panel (`.set-rows`). A change is saved at once, one field at a time; a small green mono *salvato* lights next to the title for a moment, a refusal comes back as a toast. Text fields save when they are left. A list of switches (servers, chats, channels) is a `--well` frame with a hairline between rows (`.set-switches`). On phones under 600px a rocker, a number or an hour stays beside its title; buttons, text fields and wide controls go under it. The agent's pages use the same rows (Profilo: Identità with Nome, Colore, Genere and Immagine, Ascolto with Loquacità, Canali and In onda, Voce; Cervello's *Come pensa*; the Discord, Telegram and Slack account panels, where the agent's own bot or app is a row with its steps and token under it; *Account GitHub* (owner only) with a badge for its state: *Crea l'app di <nome>* primary; then the bot's login in bold mono and *Organizzazioni*, a `--well` list (`.gh-org`) with one line per installation: a green check or an amber alert, the account in mono, the repositories and whether it is in use (amber-washed and with *Approva* when someone else installed it), and a ghost *Repo* to GitHub; under it *Installa su GitHub* (primary, the first time) or *Aggiungi un'organizzazione* (ghost) and *Controlla su GitHub*; then a line on the desktop's tokens, the agent's picture with *Scarica l'immagine* and *Apri l'app su GitHub*, and an armed *Scollega*; *Proprietario*, one row *Risponde a* with a select of the real people who wrote in the channels, grouped by platform (`optgroup` Discord, Slack, Telegram), "Te, il proprietario dello spazio" first; on a phone the select goes under its text at full width), but there the changes wait for the `.savebar`'s *Salva*: a character text must never save half typed.
- **Channel pages**: the panel header carries the channel's state badge. The first row is *Cora vive su Discord* when it is the one in use, otherwise *Canale di Cora* with *Usa Telegram* (primary, disabled until there is a token; the first tap turns it red with *Conferma: Discord si spegne*, the second moves Cora). Then the bot or app that listens (a row; while it is missing, the steps and the token form under its text). Then *Ascolto*: the servers, chats or channels as switches, and *Proprietari*. Slack adds *Aspetto delle app*, the configuration token as a row.
- **Subscriptions and keys** (the pages of *Abbonamenti* and *Chiavi API*): *Account del server* holds the resident switch as a row, *Rileva account* in the header and the server's accounts as cards that open their provider; a provider's page has *Su questo server* (its resident account) and *Altri account*: every account connected for that harness as rows in one `--well` frame between hairlines (`.account-row`: an LED — green with a glow when connected, red on a failed sign-in, amber blinking while signing in — the email or label in 15px, under it in mono whose account it is, its state and how many agents use it; on the right *Verifica* for a connected one, *Ricollega* for a failed or disconnected one, and a trash button, disabled with a reason while an agent uses it), an account being signed in opening its steps under its row on a dashed hairline, then *Aggiungi account* (primary when there is none yet) to connect one more; on phones under 480px *Ricollega* keeps only its icon. A section's title (`.set-section > .label`) reads like a row's. A key's page has its badge in the panel header, what the key is for, and the masked field with its save button as a row.
- **Brain** (`#/brain`, `.brain-*`): a main section with the bulb icon. The list page: a row of the desk's stat cards (`statCard`, as on the Banco: pages, to review — linking to the review when something waits —, searchable messages, the librarian's last run), a search field and kind chips (*Tutte*, Clienti, Progetti, Persone, Decisioni, Procedure, Temi), then `.brain-row`s between hairlines (title in the display font, summary muted, kind badge and an amber *Da rivedere* badge on the right), then the owner's *Il bibliotecario* panel in setting rows, each saved as it changes (model picker, embedding model, the librarian's hour and the review's hour as hour selects). The page header carries *Rivedi (N)* (primary while something waits, ghost otherwise) and *Aggiorna adesso*. A page: the title as page head with kind, review and updated-at, the summary as lead, the text rendered as markdown (see **Markdown**; `[[slug]]` as amber mono links; never HTML), and *Storia*: each revision with action, review badge, author and time, its note, a conflict as `.notice.warn`, and its sources as quotes with an amber left rule (who in amber mono, channel and date faint). The review: one `.brain-change` card per change (amber border when flagged), the line diff in a mono well (added lines on a faint green wash with `+`, removed ones struck through on a faint red wash with `−`), the sources, and *Annulla* (ghost) · *Approva* (primary); *Approva tutto* in the header. Nothing on the review blocks anything: the copy says changes already count. Above the search a `.segmented` switch *Elenco* · *Mappa* (`#/brain` · `#/brain/mappa`). New review cards start collapsed: `.brain-change-toggle` is a 44px keyboard-accessible title button with a chevron and `aria-expanded`; *Approva* stays visible beside the title. `.brain-change-body` holds the diff, sources, page link and revert action. *Apri tutte* / *Chiudi tutte* toggles the whole queue. The owner can add every page kind with *Aggiungi pagina* (or the selected kind) from either list or map; `#/brain/nuova/:kind` uses the established form fields and a primary save action. Manual pages apply immediately as approved knowledge. On phones the app bar names the creation action or the saved page.
- **Navigation counts** (`.nav-count`): an amber pill with ink digits and a soft glow at the end of a sidebar entry, and on the tab bar's icon on phones. It holds what waits for *this* viewer, such as the commits they may review beside **Artefatti**, and is kept in step when a page learns a newer number.
- **Artifacts** (`#/artefatti`, `.art-*`): the tape library. A main section with the archive icon.
  - **List.** The desk's stat cards (folders, to review, on air, space against the quota), then the amber `.requests` strip *Aspettano te* with each waiting commit: author, message, folder path, `+a ~m −d` counts in green, amber and red mono, a conflict badge. Under it, the folders grouped under mono caps labels by their first segment, each group a panel of `.art-row`s.
  - **A folder row.** An 8px LED (green on air, amber blinking waiting, red conflict, dark nothing on air), a folder or globe icon well, and the path in DM Mono with its parent faint and its leaf bright. Under it the title, and on the right the badges and `files · size · when` in faint mono.
  - **New folder.** An inline panel: the title first, then *Dove va*, then *Cosa ci va*, a `.segmented` *File* · *Sito*, and for the owner the *Pubblica subito* rocker.
    - **Dove va** (`folderPathPicker()`) is never typed whole. A well holds crumbs: a faint *Artefatti* root, then each chosen group as a 32px mono chip with an amber folder icon and a caret, then a dashed *+ in un gruppo* chip, then the folder's name as a borderless mono field that follows the title until edited.
    - A chip opens a menu under the crumbs (amber-lined card on `--panel-2`): the groups at that level as tiles with their folder count, a *nuovo-gruppo* field with *Nuovo gruppo*, and *Togli questo livello*. Only groups are offered; a name already a folder there is refused.
    - Under it, the whole path in amber mono with a green check, or a red line saying why it cannot be made.
  - **A folder.** The title as page head, the path and the kind and policy badges under it, the description as a muted lead. The *on air* card follows: `.art-lamp` lit red like the ON AIR lamp, but its own class, since `updateLamp()` drives every `.onair`. It shows the served commit's message in the display font, the short id in amber mono, the author and size, and *Rivedi (N)* when something waits.
  - **The folder's tabs.** A ghost *← Artefatti* button sits above the title on desktop (on a phone the app bar's chevron does it); a commit page has *← <folder>* the same way. *File* walks the tree one level at a time, the level in the address (`#/artefatti/<id>/file/<dir…>`): an upload strip on top (a dashed card on `--panel-2` with an amber upload icon well, *Trascina qui file o cartelle, oppure*, the target path as amber mono, *Carica qui* primary and *Una cartella* ghost; a non-reviewer reads *Proponi qui*; the whole tab is the drop zone and lights amber while files hover; while sending it dims, the icon blinks and a faint mono line counts files and bytes), and a ghost *Nuova cartella* that opens an inline name field under a dashed hairline and goes into that subfolder, where an empty list says it exists once its first files arrive; then mono breadcrumbs (the folder's last segment with a folder icon, then each level, the current one amber) above a mono list in a well (`..`, subfolders with an amber folder icon and their file count, then files with their size) beside an inert preview whose header has *Modifica* for Markdown, CSV and plain text (*Proponi una modifica* for a non-reviewer) and a full-screen button (the browser's own full screen, or the preview covering the page until Esc) (CSV and TSV as a table in a well that scrolls inside its frame: fixed layout, each column sized to what it holds between 80 and 360px, long cells wrapping, a grip on each heading's edge to drag the width (double click puts it back), sticky mono headers on `--panel-2`, numbers right-aligned, rows lit amber on hover, 500 rows with a count under it; Markdown rendered in a well; a PDF in a white-backed frame from the space's `r-` preview host): pictures on a checkerboard, media players, text with line numbers, everything else as *Scarica*. *Commit* is the history as takes on a tape: a dashed vertical rule, each take a ring (green published, amber blinking proposed, red conflict or rejected, the live one a lit red dot), short id, message, author, time and counts, *Rivedi* or an armed *Ripristina*. *Permissions* holds named-agent writers, individual team access and reviewers. *Settings* holds title, description, default team access, publishing policy and deletion; settings save as they change.
  - **Settings → Spazio** (owner): the used size and the free disk as two display-font figures over faint mono captions, a 10px amber meter on a well (its fill set through `--fill`, since the CSP forbids inline styles), then the setting row *Tetto per gli artefatti* with *Metà del disco · N GB* · *Un tetto mio* as a `.segmented`; the latter opens a mono GB field that saves on change.
  - **Editing a file.** The preview becomes the editor under the same header (*Modifica · path*). Markdown and text: a mono textarea, with Markdown's rendering beside it on desktop and under it on a phone. CSV: the same table, every cell a borderless mono field that grows with its text and lights amber when focused, a row number column whose ✕ removes the row on hover, and *Aggiungi riga* · *Aggiungi colonna* · *Togli l'ultima colonna* above it. Below both: *Cosa hai cambiato*, *Annulla*, *Salva*. A reviewer's save goes on air and reopens the file; anyone else's opens its review.
  - **Registro** (owner and reviewers): `.segmented` filters *Tutto* · *Visite* · *Download* · *Accessi* · *Modifiche* and a link select, then one row per event. Each row has a 30px icon well (green eye for views, amber for downloads, red for refusals), a sentence (the address bold, or "Visitatore abc123" in muted mono; files and folders in amber mono), browser · system · link in faint mono, and the time on the right. *Carica le precedenti* pages back.
  - **A subfolder's page.** Inside `dir`, the page head is the subfolder's last segment, the path line its full path, the lead "Una sottocartella di <folder>", and the ghost back button goes up one level. Every tab keeps the level (`?in=<dir>`), and the File tab's upload strip adds *Condividi questa cartella* and an armed ghost *Elimina questa cartella* (*Elimina tutto quello che contiene* once armed; *Proponi di eliminarla* for a non-reviewer), which goes up a level when done.
