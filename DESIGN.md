# Cora — Design System

The admin panel is a **radio studio mixing desk**. Each agent is a channel strip on the desk: LEDs for its state, a small monitor showing its desktop. Discord is the "ON AIR" lamp. The live activity feed is a teletype strip. Dark and tactile: one amber light on near-pure black, never brown.

Each agent also has its own `color` (stored on the agent, default `#e8a33d`), used for its picture and its name in the feed. Cora draws each agent's picture like the mark: a circle and a ring as lines in the agent's color on `#070707`, the ring's far half faint, two big eyes; the seed varies where the ring sits (visor, cap, collar), its tilt and the eyes. A drawn picture carries its own circle, so it shows with no ring around it (`.avatar.drawn`); an uploaded one gets the color ring.

## Typography

Loaded from Google Fonts.

- **Display — Outfit** (600): logo, page titles, panel titles, agent names on strips. Rounded geometric, sentence case, tight tracking (`-0.02em` small, `-0.04em` large).
- **Body — Manrope** (400/500/600): forms, paragraphs, buttons.
- **Mono — DM Mono** (400/500): cron expressions, ids, timestamps, the teletype feed, small hardware-style labels (`font-size: 10–11px`, uppercase, `letter-spacing: .12em`).

Scale: 11 / 13 / 15 (base) / 17 / 20 / 38 / 44 / 56px.

## Spacing & shape

- Spacing unit 4px; common steps 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- Radii: `--r-sm 6px` (inputs, chips), `--r-md 10px` (cards), `--r-lg 16px` (strips, panels). LEDs and avatars are circles.
- Borders are 1px `--line`; panels add an inner top highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.04)` and a soft drop shadow, like brushed hardware.
- Content max width 1280px, 16px side gutter on phones.

## Brand

- **Name:** Cora — Computer-Operating Resident Agents. Wordmark "Cora" in Outfit 600.
- **Mark:** a sphere in outline on black: a thin amber rim and one equator tilted −24°, its far half at 38% opacity for depth. No fill, no glow. Drawn by `bin/icons.mjs` (PNG app icons, supersampled) and `web/favicon.svg`.
- **Login:** the mark, the wordmark, the tagline, then the password card; nothing animated around it.

## App shell

The panel behaves like an installed app (web manifest, apple-touch-icon, no pinch or double-tap zoom, 16px inputs on phones so iOS never zooms).

- **Desktop (≥ 900px)**: a fixed sidebar (brand, the channel card with the ON AIR lamp, navigation, the agents with a status dot, and at its foot who is signed in, which opens the account menu) and a scrolling content column.
- **Phone**: a top app bar (back chevron on sub-pages, page title, and at the right end your account: a 36px round button with your mark, which opens the account menu) and a bottom tab bar (Agenti, Banco, Brain, Artefatti, Impostazioni: one row, as many equal columns as there are sections) with safe-area padding. The shell is `position: fixed` and sized to the real visible viewport via `--app-h` (set from `visualViewport` in `fitViewport()`), so iOS standalone PWAs show no dead strip at the bottom; only the content scrolls. While the keyboard is up (`.kbd-open`) the tab bar hides so the composer rests on the keyboard. Chat pages fill the screen with the composer docked above the tab bar: there the agent card is hidden (the app bar already names the agent), the working card is one line, and the tab bar keeps only the clearance the home indicator needs (`max(4px, safe-bottom - 14px)`) instead of the whole inset, so the log gets about two thirds of the screen.
- **Icons**: inline 24px stroke icons (`ICONS` in `web/app.js`, stroke 1.8, round caps), in amber icon wells (`.panel-ico`) on panel headers and stat cards; Discord is `#`, Telegram is the paper plane, Slack is four strokes turning around a centre.
- **Panels**: every section is a `.panel` with a header (icon well, title in the display font, one-line hint, actions on the right; on phones status badges drop under the title) and a body with 14px rhythm. An agent's forms end with a sticky `.savebar`; Settings has none, a setting saves as it changes.

## Components

- **Subscription quotas** (`.subscription-usage`): on Agents, share one overview slot with global token usage; a 44px icon switch in the upper right alternates the mounted views and retains the selection across live updates and reload. Use a compact horizontal icon tab rail and show exactly one account panel. Tabs have provider names, accessible account labels, roving keyboard focus and 44px touch targets; multiple accounts of a provider show their email or account name. Show only configured accounts used by visible agents, once per account; omit the section when none qualify. Identify assigned agents in the selected account header. Keep the selected tab and mounted wells through unrelated live updates. Black quota wells show model/pool, remaining percentage explicitly labelled *available*, a native progress meter, allowance window and reset time. Ollama includes monthly allowance and purchased credits; paid API tabs such as DeepSeek show monetary balances by currency, including total, granted and topped-up amounts, without progress bars or invented reset times. Space account balances are visible only to space owners. Separate session, weekly, monthly and model-specific pools, including Fable and Argon. Green indicates available allowance, amber at most 20% remaining, red exhaustion; old readings use neutral meters and a stale note. The backend polls once per minute independently of open browsers; the panel reads snapshots and updates changed values in place, without repeated loading indicators. Initial loading uses the neutral spinner. Missing percentages stay unknown and cooldowns show the next permitted backend check.
- **Usage heatmaps**: show the first recorded usage day through today, at most the year returned by the API. Each agent starts at its own first use. Week alignment uses invisible placeholders, never dated cells before the first record or in the future. Keep squares at 14px; show the date range with its year. Scroll long histories to the latest week only after mounting, and realign when the viewport changes. Show an explicit empty state when there is no recorded usage.
- **Home**: opening the panel at `/` or `#/` shows Agents. One global overview comes first, switching between token consumption and quotas, followed by the agent roster and individual token usage. Keep New agent in the roster header (a labelled + on phones); omit the redundant on-air count and mobile page-title row. The lounge has its own `#/salotto` route, also used by lounge requests and push notifications, but no menu entry: it opens from the Banco's *Salotto* button, the Banco entry stays lit while it is open, and on a phone its back chevron returns to the Banco. Explicit links to agent tabs still open the requested tab.
- **Boot outline**: while the state first loads, the shell draws the page in the address (`bootSkeleton()`): its own title and the sidebar entry lit, plain rows, the chat outline for the lounge and an agent; the agents page keeps its usage and agent-card outline and the chosen overview: a heatmap or the compact quota tabs, selected account header and two quota wells.
- **Skeletons**: neutral placeholders use the existing panel and line tokens with a quiet neutral pulse, never amber. Match the content shape: avatar and text rows, usage heatmaps, and alternating chat bubbles with a composer. Loading regions use `role="status"`, a translated loading label and `aria-busy="true"`; visual shapes are hidden from assistive technology. Replace placeholders with content or a concrete error, and respect reduced motion.

- **Token forms**: one password input and a «Collega» button in an `.input-row.token`. A service that needs two tokens (Slack: the bot's and the app-level one) stacks both inputs in a `.stack-sm` with the button on its own row, and says in the steps which page of the provider each one comes from.
- **Paste field** (`pasteField()`, `.paste-field`): every input filled by pasting (subscription authorization codes, API keys, bot and configuration tokens, vault values, external links) has a ghost «Incolla» button with the clipboard icon beside it, which reads the clipboard into the field; it is omitted where the browser has no clipboard reading, and a refused or empty clipboard gives a toast and focuses the field. The button that sends the value (*Completa collegamento*, *Salva chiave*, *Collega*…) stays disabled while the field is empty; a check of something saved (*Verifica collegamento*) appears only once there is something saved to check.
- **Manifest block**: for a provider that creates its apps from a document (Slack), a row with «Copia il manifest» and the link that opens the provider, and a `<details>` holding the JSON in the mono font. Never make someone retype what a paste can do.
- **Telegram group guide** (`.telegram-setup`): inside Account Telegram, a group select and *Verifica passaggi* lead into five rows separated by hairlines. A 28px mono number becomes a green check only after actual evidence; the first unfinished step is amber, later steps neutral. Each row names its outcome and the next action, with links to BotFather, the group invitation, Settings or the agent's Listening and its own *Ho fatto, verifica* button. Status text distinguishes unchecked, incomplete and verified; errors clear previous success instead of implying it is current. The final step gives a copyable message with a unique test code for the person to send and distinguishes reception from the correlated delivered reply. Retain the re-invite instruction after a privacy change even when membership is confirmed. Keep controls at least 44px tall, wrap titles and buttons on phones, and update only the guide so profile drafts survive checks.

- **Your account** (`accountButton()`, `.profile-*`): the signed-in person's mark (`.user-mark`: the picture their sign-in gave, else their initials in amber DM Mono on `--panel-2`) in a round button at the phone top bar's right end, and their name and email at the sidebar's foot with a chevron. Either opens one menu, a `--panel` card with a `--line-strong` hairline and a deep shadow, below the button on a phone (over a 45% black scrim) and above the foot on a desktop (300px wide): the mark, name in the display font, email in mono and the role badge (*Owner* / *Utente*), then *Accesso*, *Lingua e notifiche*, a hairline and *Esci*. *Esci* never signs out at once: the menu turns into *Vuoi uscire?* with one line on what follows (on usecora.space you also leave the cloud and sign in again; locally the password is needed again), *Annulla* (ghost) and *Esci* (primary). Esc, a tap outside, navigation and a resize close the menu; opened from the keyboard it takes the focus.
- **ON AIR lamp** (`.onair`): in the sidebar's channel card on desktop; phones show a channel's state in Settings, and the top bar's right end is your account. Lit red with glow when Discord is `ready`, amber blinking while connecting, dark when off/error (error text in the tooltip and the dashboard).
- **Channel strip** (`.strip`): agent card on the dashboard. Top: monitor (desktop thumbnail, 16:10, scanline overlay, "SPENTO" when off). Middle: avatar (its color ring, or the drawn picture's own circle), name (display font), model (mono). Bottom: LEDs (`.led` on/off/warn) for *attivo*, *desktop*, *strumenti*. Whole strip is a link to the agent.
- **Character sheet** (`.character-*`): the six-trait identity in Carattere. Six trait faders in a two-column grid — score in mono amber, poles in faint mono — two style faders, type/style badges, and the generated prompt in a black `.character-preview` well. The fader is the desk's amber cap on a ticked track; the preview is the exact text the server prompts with. One column on phones.
- **Teletype** (`.teletype`): paper strip with perforation edges, mono text, newest on top, each line `time · who · what`; tool calls in blue, errors in red, agent messages in the agent's color.
- **Tabs** (`.tabs`): hardware buttons; active one is lit amber with a small LED dot. Scroll horizontally on phones.
- **Buttons**: `.btn` (panel-2, line border), `.btn.primary` (amber fill, ink text), `.btn.danger` (red outline), `.btn.ghost`. 36px tall, 44px on touch.
- **Toggle** (`.switch`): small rocker switch with LED.
- **Model picker** (`.model-pick`): the model list grouped by provider, and under it a mono text box for any `provider/model` id; when the box is filled it wins and the list dims. Used while an agent is being created, where there is no Brain tab yet.
- **Agent Brain** (`#/agent/:id/cervello/:section`): use the same `sectionWorkspace()` rail as Agent Settings, Memory and Email: **Model and account**, **Reasoning**, **Computer**. Keep all panes mounted and associate their fields with one shared native form; its savebar stays in the detail column. The model pane uses setting rows with a harness select and a one-line explanation, followed by the account and models. Unavailable harnesses remain discoverable with their reason. Preserve subscription authorization drafts and model selections when changing sections. Local and cloud choices, hardware verdicts, missing-harness notices and the custom model disclosure retain their existing behavior. Phones show the section list or one pane with a local Brain back action. Validation failures open the model pane.
- **Notice** (`.notice`, `.notice.warn`): an inline explanation inside a panel, icon left and text right; the warn variant is amber-lined on an amber wash. For a condition the reader must act on, never for status.
- **Subscription picker** (`.subscription-picker`): a quiet account card with a provider mark, identity and a text status with an LED. The default resident account is identified explicitly; **Change account** reveals selectable account rows and **Connect another account**. Browser authorization replaces the card body with two numbered steps; device codes use a dashed mono well with a copy button, while Claude's returned code goes in a password field. Show errors inline, keep entered codes across polls and failed requests, and place advanced device-code help behind a disclosure. Every pending action shows a neutral rotating ring and a translated operation label with a polite status announcement; respect reduced motion. During authorization verification retain and disable the code input and submit button until a final result, with cancellation available. Protect preparation requests from page reconstruction as well as mounted authorization steps. Avoid showing creation inputs, selection controls and login actions simultaneously. The same component serves agent forms and Settings, with context-appropriate copy.
- **Settings** (`.rail`): one list on the left (272px, a `--panel` card whose items scroll inside it, so its 8px margin never scrolls away) and the page of the entry in the address on the right (`#/settings/<entry>`). The list is grouped under mono caps labels: *Canali* (Discord, Telegram, Slack), *Intelligenza* (*Account del server*, Claude, ChatGPT / Codex, and Grok and Meta once installed or connected, followed by *Chiavi API* with Ollama, OrcaRouter, DeepSeek, TypeSafe and Soniox on one page), *Agenti* (Regia, Modelli e sonno, Domini), *Persone* (Accesso, Chiavi SSH), *Generale* (Controlli di doc, Lingua e notifiche). Each entry is a 30px icon well, the name in 14px Manrope with its state in faint mono under it ("in uso · in onda", "2 account", "Chiave salvata", "11 messaggi · obiettivi accesi", "solo tu"), and an 8px LED for the entries that can work or not: green when it works here, amber for a program missing, a sign-in in progress or a default model that does not answer, red for a channel in error, dark otherwise; entries with nothing to light keep the space empty. The chosen entry is lit like the sidebar: `--panel-2` (no coloured side bar anywhere: selection is shown by the background and amber text only), the name and its icon well amber, and the list scrolls to keep it in view. Without an entry in the address a desktop opens the primary platform. A platform's page starts with whether Cora is there: off, an amber *Accendi <platform>* (it joins the others); on with others, *Rendi principale* (ghost) and *Spegni* (armed twice); the primary says that Cora's private messages to the owner arrive there. Agent cards and the agent header carry one account badge per platform the agent is on (*Discord · Bot suo*), or *Solo pannello*. On phones the list comes first, full width, each entry with a chevron; an entry opens its page alone, with *‹ Impostazioni* above it. The old tab addresses (`collegamenti/<id>`, `canali`, `agenti`…) open their page. Every page stays mounted, so a sign-in half done survives a switch. On a desktop the page fits the screen (`.mode-rail`): the title stays, the list and the entry's page each scroll in their own column, the page returns to its top when the entry changes, and the margins above and below never scroll away; on phones the page scrolls as a whole.
- **New agent** (`#/new`): identity, character, listening and voice, followed by *Create agent*. Brain and subscription selection live only in the saved agent’s Brain tab; creation uses the instance default model and the normal computer default.
- **Setting rows** (`.set-row`, `setRow()`): the grammar of every settings page.
